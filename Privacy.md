# FieldOps AI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 21, 2026

FieldOps AI ("FieldOps AI," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application operated by an independent developer.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, stored, and shared when you use the FieldOps AI mobile application and its related services.

By using FieldOps AI, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information FieldOps AI Collects

Depending on the features you use, FieldOps AI may collect or process the following information.

### Account Information

If you create an account, FieldOps AI may collect information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Account identifier
* Authentication information
* Company or organization name, if voluntarily provided

### Equipment Information

When using FieldOps AI to identify or service equipment, the application may collect or process information including:

* Equipment manufacturer
* Equipment model
* Equipment type
* Serial numbers or equipment identifiers
* Equipment labels and markings
* Industry or equipment category
* Equipment photographs
* Service and maintenance information

### Job and Service Information

Information associated with service jobs may include:

* Job descriptions
* Job status
* Technician notes
* Inspection findings
* Service history
* Maintenance actions
* Recommendations
* Equipment information
* Customer or job-site information entered by the user
* Photographs or other information uploaded by the user

## 2. Camera and Photo Access

FieldOps AI may request access to your device's camera or photo library.

Camera access is used for features such as:

* Photographing equipment
* Scanning equipment
* Identifying equipment
* Reading equipment labels or markings
* Attaching photographs to service records

FieldOps AI does not access your camera unless permission has been granted through your device.

You may disable camera or photo access at any time through your device's privacy settings. Certain equipment-scanning or documentation features may not function when these permissions are disabled.

## 3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

FieldOps AI uses artificial intelligence to provide features including:

* Equipment identification
* Equipment classification
* Extraction of equipment information from photographs
* Analysis of user-provided equipment information
* Generation of service summaries
* Generation of findings
* Generation of recommendations
* Generation of service reports

When you intentionally use an AI-powered feature, information necessary to perform the requested operation may be transmitted to third-party artificial intelligence or cloud-processing service providers.

This information may include equipment photographs, equipment information, technician notes, job information, or other content you choose to submit to an AI-powered feature.

FieldOps AI does not sell user-submitted equipment photographs, service records, or technician notes to third parties for advertising purposes.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information collected or processed by FieldOps AI may be used to:

* Operate and provide the application
* Authenticate users
* Identify equipment
* Create and manage service jobs
* Maintain equipment and service histories
* Store technician notes and findings
* Generate AI-assisted reports
* Generate recommendations
* Synchronize information between authorized devices
* Provide technical support
* Maintain application security
* Diagnose errors and technical problems
* Improve application functionality and reliability
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

## 5. AI-Generated Information

Artificial intelligence can make mistakes.

Equipment identification, technical information, service summaries, findings, and recommendations generated by FieldOps AI should be independently verified by a qualified person before being relied upon.

FieldOps AI is intended to assist technicians and other qualified users and is not a substitute for professional judgment, manufacturer documentation, approved maintenance information, safety procedures, or regulatory requirements.

## 6. Aviation and Safety-Critical Use

FieldOps AI may be used with aviation, HVAC, industrial, mechanical, electrical, and other equipment.

When used in aviation or another safety-critical environment, information generated by FieldOps AI is provided as an informational and organizational aid.

FieldOps AI does not provide:

* Airworthiness approval
* Return-to-service authorization
* Engineering approval
* Regulatory approval
* Approved maintenance data
* Manufacturer authorization
* Certification that equipment is safe to operate

Users are responsible for following applicable regulations, manufacturer documentation, approved maintenance manuals, company procedures, safety requirements, and other authoritative technical information.

A qualified technician or other appropriately authorized person should verify information before performing maintenance or making safety-critical decisions.

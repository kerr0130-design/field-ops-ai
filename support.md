## Equipment Scanning

If FieldOps AI is unable to identify equipment, make sure:

* The equipment label or data plate is clearly visible.
* The photograph is well lit.
* Important model and manufacturer information is in focus.
* The camera lens is clean and unobstructed.
* FieldOps AI has permission to access your device's camera.

AI-generated equipment identification may not always be accurate. Always verify equipment information against the manufacturer's identification plate, documentation, or other authoritative information before performing service or maintenance.

## AI-Generated Information

FieldOps AI uses artificial intelligence to assist with equipment identification, service documentation, findings, recommendations, and report generation.

AI-generated information should be reviewed and verified by a qualified technician before it is relied upon.

For aviation or other safety-critical applications, FieldOps AI does not replace approved maintenance data, manufacturer documentation, regulatory requirements, engineering approvals, or qualified professional judgment.
